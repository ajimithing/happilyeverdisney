About

Happily Ever is a high-energy tribute to the Disney songs that defined generations. The anthems, the sing-alongs, and the ballads that soundtracked a childhood, then somehow never really left. From “Under the Sea” to “Let It Go” to “We Don't Talk About Bruno”, the band brings decades of Disney music to the stage with a complete full-band lineup.

Audiences are transported straight into the heart of Disney’s most beloved music. With powerhouse vocals, tight musicianship, and feel-good energy that fills the room, Happily Ever creates an unforgettable evening of nostalgia and celebration where every generation sings along together.
